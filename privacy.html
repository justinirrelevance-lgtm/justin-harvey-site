Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy covers a personal automation tool built and operated solely by Justin Harvey to manage his own YouTube channel and the "In My Shoes" podcast. It is not a public-facing service, has no other users, and does not collect information from visitors to this website.

Information Accessed

The tool uses Google OAuth to access the YouTube Data API for Justin Harvey's own YouTube channel only. This includes:

  • A list of the channel's videos, including private and scheduled uploads
  • Video metadata: titles, descriptions, hashtags, privacy status, and scheduling information

No data belonging to any other YouTube user, channel, or viewer is accessed at any point.

How Information Is Used

Access is used exclusively to automate uploading, scheduling, and updating bilingual (Korean/English) titles and descriptions for the operator's own video content. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating this personal tool, and is never sold or shared with any third party.

Data Storage & Retention

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the operator's own device and are used only to authenticate requests to Google's APIs. No data collected through this access is transmitted to, or stored by, any third-party service. Access can be revoked at any time via the operator's Google Account permissions, at which point stored credentials become unusable.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service involved is the Google/YouTube Data API, governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to justin.irrelevance@gmail.com.
